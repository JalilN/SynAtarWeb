A. Why Value Recovery?

Value Recovery provides a focused review of subsurface knowledge, operational data and development options to uncover opportunities that may not be visible in routine asset reviews. It helps teams challenge assumptions, reduce uncertainty and make more informed decisions that improve technical and commercial outcomes.

Back to How the methodology works

B. How the methodology works

The approach integrates multidisciplinary technical review, data-led diagnosis and opportunity screening. Existing evidence is assessed, knowledge gaps are identified, and potential recovery options are ranked against value, feasibility, risk and the confidence of supporting information.

Back to Where can it be applied?

C. Where can it be applied?

Value Recovery can support producing fields, undeveloped discoveries, mature assets, acquired portfolios, carbon storage projects and geothermal developments. It is suited to any project where additional subsurface understanding could unlock production, resource, schedule or cost improvements.

Back to What does the client receive?

D. What does the client receive?

Clients receive a concise technical report, opportunity register and prioritised recommendations. Findings are presented in a clear decision context, with uncertainties, dependencies and proposed next steps defined so the client can take focused action.
