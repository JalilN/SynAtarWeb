Who is responsible

SynAtar is a UK-based consultancy (Company Registration No. 17200144) and is responsible for personal information submitted through this website. For privacy questions, please use the website contact form.

Information we collect

When you contact us through the website, we collect your name, email address and message. Company name and nature of enquiry are optional. The form also sends the page URL and includes a hidden anti-spam field. Do not include sensitive personal information in your message.

How we use your information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, discuss requested services and manage follow-up. We rely on our legitimate interests in handling business enquiries and, where relevant, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We also use the form's anti-spam measure to help protect the website from automated submissions.

Form provider and sharing

Contact form submissions are sent through FormSubmit, a third-party form handling service, which receives the information entered in the form and relays it to SynAtar. Review FormSubmit's current privacy information for details of its processing. Before publishing this policy, SynAtar should confirm the provider's terms and any applicable international transfer safeguards.

We may also share information where necessary with professional advisers or public authorities where the law requires it.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond, manage any resulting business relationship, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. The specific retention period should be confirmed and documented before this draft is published.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict or object to its use, and request a copy in a portable format. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details above. You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or data handling practices change. The published version should include the date it was last reviewed.
