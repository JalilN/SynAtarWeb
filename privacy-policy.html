Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

SynAtar Ltd is a UK-based geoscience and technical advisory consultancy (Company Registration No. 17200144) and is the data controller responsible for personal information collected through this website.

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us through our Contact page.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us through our website, we collect your name, email address and enquiry message, together with your company name and nature of enquiry, where provided.

Limited technical information may also be processed to support website functionality, security and spam prevention.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information or confidential information in your initial enquiry.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests for information.
  • Communicate about our services and potential projects.
  • Establish and maintain professional and business relationships.
  • Explore potential business and collaboration opportunities.
  • Meet applicable legal and contractual obligations.

We process personal information primarily on the basis of our legitimate business interests, provided these do not override your rights and freedoms. Where applicable, we may also process information to fulfil contractual or legal obligations or with your consent.

4. Third-party services and information sharing

Our website uses GitHub Pages for website hosting, FormSubmit for processing website enquiries and Microsoft 365 for business email and communications.

These service providers may process personal information as part of delivering their services, including processing or storing information outside the United Kingdom.

Further information about their data handling practices is available through their respective privacy policies:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • FormSubmit Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement

We may also share information with professional advisers or public authorities where necessary to meet legal or business obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. How long we keep information

We may retain your name, email address and company name for future professional and business communications, maintaining business relationships and exploring potential opportunities for collaboration.

We retain personal information for as long as it remains relevant and necessary for these purposes or to meet applicable legal and contractual obligations.

We periodically review the information we hold and securely remove personal information that is no longer required.

6. Your rights

Under applicable data protection legislation, you may have the right to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict or object to its processing, request data portability and withdraw consent where applicable.

You have the right to object to direct marketing at any time.

To exercise your rights or update your communication preferences, please contact us through our Contact page.

You also have the right to raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business activities, website functionality, data handling practices or applicable legislation.

The latest version will be available on this website.
